ABOUT THIS PORTFOLIO

A huge shout-out to Thoma Lecornu,

who jumped onto this project with me.

His personality and determination

were, for sure, one of the key elements

in the success of this portfolio.

And, a big shout-out to Victor, Félix

and Lucas for their patience and 3D

crazy skills !

My goal was to strike the right balance between an immersive 3D experience and a clean 2D environment, allowing visitors to explore my projects in a single scroll and understand my skill set in under 10 seconds.

I built the concept around the moustache daisy, a symbol that reflects both my personality and my creative approach. As the experience unfolds, you'll discover a character meticulously sculpted in an artist's workshop, one that exudes the beauty of nature, or one that becomes a strange and playful magical object.
